You do not need to know every tool in a group's name before asking whether a meetup is right for you. Read the group's description, look at its upcoming events, and contact the organizer if experience requirements are unclear.

The directory below uses current SpokaneTech listings. Groups set their own membership and participation requirements; a listing does not guarantee that every event is open to everyone.

Check before you go.

Follow the event's RSVP link to confirm the current time, venue or online format, cost, and attendance requirements. Event details and cancellations can change. Ask the organizer about accessibility, parking, and anything you need to bring.

If you are new, consider introducing yourself to an organizer and asking how the meeting works. Participation and networking styles vary; choose what feels comfortable for you.

Meeting schedules, beginner friendliness, accessibility, and membership rules vary. Contact the organizer to confirm those details. If a group has no upcoming event here, its profile may still link to ways to learn more.
