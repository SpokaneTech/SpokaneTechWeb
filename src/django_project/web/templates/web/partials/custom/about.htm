About SpokaneTech.org

Connecting people who build, learn, and explore technology in Spokane and the Inland Northwest.

One community. Many technologies.

A place for everyone in tech.

SpokaneTech.org brings together technology enthusiasts across Spokane and the greater Inland Northwest. Our mission is to build community, share knowledge, and help people grow.

Founded on collaboration and innovation, our community welcomes tech professionals, hobbyists, students, and anyone with an interest in technology. Working together and sharing what we know creates opportunities for personal and professional growth and helps our region move forward.

Connect, learn, and share.

Meetups, workshops, and events give members a place to connect, learn, and share their experiences. Topics range from software development and cybersecurity to emerging technologies like AI and blockchain. Whether you're a seasoned professional or just starting your tech journey, there's a place for you here.
